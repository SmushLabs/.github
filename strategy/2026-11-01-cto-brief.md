## Competitor Gap Analysis — what SmushChat is missing vs. the field

### Must-add in next 60 days (table-stakes, you're falling behind)

1. **Voice mode (STT + TTS).** OpenAI Whisper for input, ElevenLabs or OpenAI TTS for output. Mobile users and accessibility.
2. **Multi-channel inbox: Instagram DM + Facebook Messenger + WhatsApp + web chat in one view.** DM Champ ships this at $59 LTD — don't let the gap persist.
3. **Live cart awareness for WooCommerce + Shopify.** "I see you have the Blue Hoodie in your cart — want help with sizing?" This is one API call away and converts.
4. **Human handoff with mobile push to founder.** When the AI stalls, phone buzzes. Converts to Slack + email fallback.
5. **Scheduled proactive messages based on page time/scroll depth.** Already in the roadmap as "advanced user activity detection" — bump priority.

### Differentiators to lean into (your moat)

1. **White-label + sub-accounts for agencies.** You already have this. Market it as a product: **"SmushChat for Agencies"** page, flat $99/mo for unlimited client bots. Agencies become your best resellers.
2. **Train on PDF + website + custom text in minutes.** Keep this friction-free. Add Google Drive/Notion/Confluence connectors — most competitors still make you upload manually.
3. **"Vote on features" roadmap.** Make this a *public* page, not a Notion doc. Every vote should drop the visitor's email → cheapest lead gen on the planet.
4. **Transparent per-month pricing with no per-conversation fees.** Hammer this on the landing page with the Fin / Tidio / Chatbase comparison.

### Fresh bets to prototype (90-day horizon)

1. **MCP server + client.** Position SmushChat as "the only AI chat widget that can read Shopify, Linear, HubSpot without integrations." Open-source the MCP connectors on GitHub for SEO.
2. **"Chatbot that drafts its own training data."** After 7 days live, the AI inspects every conversation it failed on and auto-proposes new training entries for owner approval. No competitor is doing this well yet.
3. **"Install from Chrome extension."** Agency owners paste a URL, SmushChat scrapes, trains, and gives them an embed in 90 seconds. Shareable screencast = viral demo.
4. **White-glove "we train your bot for you" tier at $499 one-time.** For founders who want it done. 60% margin, generates testimonials.
5. **A/B test variants on the widget itself.** Try 2 greeting messages, SmushChat shows you which converts. No one else does this.

1. **Never link to smushlabs.ai in the post.** Links kill reach on LinkedIn and X. Put the link in a reply or in bio.
2. **One idea per post.** Thread it if it needs more.
3. **Lead with a hook that is a complete sentence.** The first line is the whole bet.
4. **End with a question.** Comments are the only metric that triggers distribution.

## Marketing & Engagement Tactics (ordered by ROI)

### High-ROI, do this quarter

1. **AppSumo / Dealify / PitchGround lifetime deal.** Realistic path to 500–1500 customers in 60 days. Use proceeds to fund infra scale. Negotiate 70/30 or 60/40. Downside: refund rate 20–30%, support load. Mitigate with required onboarding call.

6. **ProductHunt launch** — only after you have 20 strong testimonials and a lifetime deal in hand to convert day-of visitors.

## What You're Missing That I'd Add

1. **No public changelog.** Fix: `changelog.smushlabs.ai` or a page on main site. Weekly entries, each with a GIF. Doubles as social content.
2. **No public status page.** Fix: cheap one on BetterUptime or Instatus. Signals maturity to prospects.
3. **No OSS presence under `smushlabs` org on GitHub.** Fix: open-source one small, useful piece — e.g. a free `smushchat-widget-lite` embed, or your WordPress plugin wrapper. Devs trust orgs with public repos.
4. **No creator/affiliate program.** Fix: 25% recurring for the first year. Give power users a reason to tweet about you.
5. **No "SmushChat on SmushChat."** Fix: eat your own dog food — the chat widget on lander.smushlabs.ai should itself be SmushChat, trained on the entire product docs. If a visitor asks it "how is this different from Intercom?", the answer *is* the sales pitch.
6. **No AI eval harness.** Fix (CTO hat): before any model swap or prompt change, run against a 50-conversation regression set. One bad deploy to a flagship customer and you lose them. Set this up before you have 100 customers.
7. **No conversation review inbox for owners.** Fix: a daily digest email with the 5 "most interesting" conversations the AI had, flagged by the AI itself. Owners actually open these. Doubles as a retention mechanism.
