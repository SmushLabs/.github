# SmushChat CTO Update — 2026-11-06

**Headline move this week:** Ship **Agentic Actions for Shopify** (bot can lookup, refund, re-ship, update subscription on its own). This is the single biggest gap vs. Intercom Fin and Ada today, and the one feature that lets you charge $79–$149/mo instead of being priced against Tidio's $29. Everything else below sits around this spine.

## 1. Top 3 Priorities (Next 2 Weeks)

1. **Agentic Actions v1 (Shopify + WooCommerce)**: `refund_order`, `get_order_status`, `cancel_subscription`, `update_address`, `send_tracking`. Users whitelist which actions the bot can take autonomously vs. require human approval. This unlocks a new pricing tier.
2. **Interactive demo bot on the landing page** — trained on SmushChat's own docs, answering "how do I install this?" and "how is this different from Tidio?" live. Highest-converting single change you can make.
3. **One case study + one metric wall**. The TEFL Group story is great; you need 2–3 more written up with the same format (traffic, % deflection, $ saved). Below the fold, a live counter: "X,XXX conversations handled this week."

## 3. Daily Social Playbook (Founder Attention, Not Selling)

**Rule of thumb:** 4 build-in-public posts : 1 soft pitch. Founders smell sales a mile off. Give away what others charge for.

### LinkedIn (1/day, M–F, ~200 words)
- Mon: **Metric Monday** — one real number from the business ("68% deflection at TEFL Group — here's what that saved them in hours"). No CTA.
- Tue: **Teardown Tuesday** — screenshot of a competitor bot failing, explain *why* it failed (missing context, bad retrieval, no action capability).
- Wed: **Build log** — what you shipped this week, what broke, what you learned. Pictures of Linear/GitHub screenshots work.
- Thu: **Opinion piece** — "Why every e-commerce chatbot is leaking leads" / "The real cost of Intercom when you hit $1M ARR"
- Fri: **Founder story** — 1 lesson from building SmushLabs. Vulnerable beats polished.

### Twitter/X (2–3/day)
- Morning: a one-line hot take or stat
- Midday: a thread (5–8 tweets) — teardowns and "I spent 10 hours so you don't have to" threads perform best
- Evening: engage with 10 founder accounts in your space (reply > post for growth under 5k followers)

### Facebook (1/day, groups > page)
Post in e-commerce and Shopify founder groups. Lead with questions, not announcements. "Anyone else frustrated with Intercom's per-seat pricing?" opens conversations; "Introducing SmushChat" gets ignored.

### Cross-platform template (reusable)
```
Hook: [number or counter-intuitive claim]
Context: [1–2 sentences on why this matters]
Story: [the thing you learned/built/saw]
Takeaway: [the actionable insight]
Soft close: [no link; maybe "building this at SmushLabs" in bio]
```

## 5. Feature Ideas (Prioritized)

### Must-ship (next 90 days)
- **Agentic Actions** (see priority 1) — Shopify first, WooCommerce second, generic HTTP webhook third
- **Voice mode / AI phone agent** — same KB, handles inbound calls. Twilio integration. Massive market in late 2026.
- **Email autoresponder brain** — same KB answers support@ inbox. One brain, multiple channels.
- **Multi-language auto-detect** — critical for global e-commerce; competitors nickel-and-dime for this
- **Scripted flows + LLM hybrid** — strict decision trees for high-stakes topics (refund policy, pricing), LLM for everything else

### Should-ship (90–180 days)
- **Agent handoff with AI summary** — when a human takes over, AI writes a one-paragraph summary of context, sentiment, and what's been tried
- **Bot test suite** — upload 50 example questions, run them against new bot versions, see diff in answers before deploying
- **Proactive outbound** — trigger a chat based on user behavior (3 pages, 2 min idle, exit intent)
- **Conversation quality scoring** — the bot rates itself, flags low-confidence answers for KB improvement
- **Reseller portal** — sub-accounts, sub-billing, logo-only branding per end customer

### Nice-to-have (180+)
- Slack/Teams integration (surface conversations internally)
- Mobile SDK (iOS/Android for in-app support)
- Model choice: Claude Haiku/Sonnet, GPT, Gemini — per-workspace. Founders love being able to pick.
- Compliance pack: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA BAA for health use cases

## 6. Competitor Research — November 2026

| Tool | Their strength | Their weakness (your wedge) |
|---|---|---|
| **Intercom Fin** | Brand, deep inbox | $0.99/resolution is predatory at scale; locks you to Intercom's platform |
| **Zendesk AI** | Enterprise distribution | SMB-hostile pricing, slow to ship AI features |
| **Tidio Lyro** | Shopify ecosystem, affordable | Weak retrieval, no agentic actions, limited KB size |
| **Chatbase** | Developer-friendly, fast | Not for non-technical founders; no CRM/order integrations |
| **Ada** | Strong enterprise AI | Multi-$10k ACV only; not for SMB |
| **Crisp** | Good design, Euro market | Weaker AI reasoning; actions are shallow |
| **ManyChat** | Flow-builder dominance | Flow-based, not LLM-first; losing to Lyro in Shopify |
| **Botpress** | Developer power | Agency/dev audience, not founder-friendly |

**What you're missing vs. the field:**
- **Agentic actions** (Fin, Ada have these; you don't yet) ← highest-priority gap
- **Voice** (Air.ai, Retell AI, PolyAI own this now; most support chat tools haven't crossed over)
- **Per-conversation audit trail** (compliance and B2B need this)
- **Official Shopify app listing** (direct install from Shopify App Store = free distribution)

**What you can add that nobody else does well:**
- **Founder-visible pricing** — a public "what this really costs at your size" calculator with no sales call required
- **Multi-brand from one login** — agency and holding-company use case is underserved
- **Lifetime deal tier on AppSumo** — one time, limited seats, generates cash + case studies fast. Controversial but highly effective for indie SaaS in this price range.
- **"Bring your own model"** — power users love it, no one else allows it cleanly
- **Open benchmark** — publish a public leaderboard of "chatbot deflection rates" by category (ecommerce / SaaS / edu). You become the reference; competitors have to opt in or look scared.

## 7. One Decision for You This Week

**Agentic actions tier pricing.** Pick one:
- **(A)** Fold into existing plans (growth-friendly, slower revenue)
- **(B)** New "Pro/Agent" tier at $149/mo with actions enabled (clean segmentation, immediate ARPU lift)
- **(C)** Usage-based: $0.05 per autonomous action taken (aligns incentives; harder to forecast)

My recommendation: **(B)** — the market is already primed by Intercom Fin's per-resolution pricing to accept a tier bump when AI *does something*, not just *says something*. Keep the base plan at today's price so you don't alienate existing customers.
